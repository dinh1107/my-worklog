# EVENTS

Alongside studying and practicing AWS services, actively participating in technology events has been an important part of developing my knowledge and professional skills. Community meetups not only help me stay informed about new trends in cloud computing and artificial intelligence but also provide practical perspectives from professionals who are directly designing and implementing solutions in real-world organizations.

By attending the **AWS Vietnam Community Meetup**, I had the opportunity to:

- Explore current trends in AI, Generative AI, and AI Agents on AWS.
- Learn about AI Agent architectures, from single-agent solutions to systems in which multiple agents collaborate.
- Better understand how to select an architecture based on business requirements, cost, latency, and the required level of control.
- Gain practical insights into the use of AI in software development, infrastructure operations, and business value creation.
- Recognize the importance of security, observability, and human oversight when deploying AI Agents.
- Exchange knowledge and expand my professional network within the AWS Vietnam community.

The event report is organized into the following sections:

- Event information and objectives.
- Agenda and presentation sessions.
- Event speakers.
- Key technical highlights.
- Knowledge and lessons learned.
- Personal reflections after the event.
- Related photos and materials.
